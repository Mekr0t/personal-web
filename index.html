02 Heuristics

How I work

4 rules I actually follow

Ship the whole pipeline.

I'd rather have one complete system than four impressive fragments. Prototype, interface, integration, deploy — the interesting engineering is in making them hold together, not in proving any one piece alone.

Debug at the boundaries.

The expensive bugs live where two systems meet. I instrument the seams first and trace the whole flow before I start changing things in the middle.

Local-first, no black boxes.

I want to be able to open the thing that broke. In practice that means fewer dependencies, and actually understanding the ones I keep.

Small tools, sharp edges.

Clear scope beats broad scope. I'd rather a tool do one job precisely than ten jobs approximately.
